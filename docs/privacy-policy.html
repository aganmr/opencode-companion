Privacy Policy — Opencode Companion

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Opencode Companion (the “App”) is an unofficial companion app for opencode. It is an independent community project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or maintained by Anomaly Innovations, Inc. or the OpenCode team. All product names, logos, and brands mentioned here are the property of their respective owners and are used for identification purposes only. This policy explains what the App does with data.

Short version

The App does not collect, transmit, store on servers, or share any personal data. There is no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Everything the App does happens on your device.

What the App does on your device

  • Server list: the origins (host + port) of servers you scan or add are saved in local app storage on your phone so you can reconnect without scanning a QR code again. This list never leaves your device.
  • Session cookies: when you pair with an opencode server by scanning its QR code, the App’s web view stores a session cookie for that server, exactly like a browser would. The cookie is sent only to the server you chose, and is kept in the App’s local web view storage.
  • Donation QR image: if you choose to save the GCash donation QR code, the App writes a static image (bundled with the App, not generated from your data) to your photo gallery. On Android 9–12 this requires the storage permission, which is requested only for that action.

What the App does NOT do

  • No personal data is collected: no accounts, no email addresses, no device identifiers, no location, no contacts, no usage analytics.
  • No data is sent to any third party. The only network traffic is the direct connection between your phone and the opencode server you explicitly scan or add.
  • No in-app purchases. The donation options (Ko-fi link, GCash QR) are informational: they open external services where you can optionally make a donation. Nothing is processed inside the App.

Data shared with the server you connect to

The App is a client for your own opencode server. When you connect, the server you chose receives the same information any browser would send (requests, the session cookie you created by scanning its QR code). What that server stores is governed by that server’s own operator, not by this policy.

Children’s privacy

The App is a developer tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated date above will be updated and the change will be noted in the app’s release notes.
